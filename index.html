Frequently Asked Questions

How is WPM calculated?

WPM is calculated as the number of correctly typed characters divided by 5 (the average word length), divided by the time taken in minutes. Typing errors reduce your final WPM.

What happens if I finish before the timer runs out?

If you complete the passage before a countdown timer ends, the result is marked "Finished Early" and shows how many seconds you had to spare.

Can I practice with my own text?

Yes. Choose "Custom Text" to paste your own paragraph, or upload a .txt file and it will be loaded as your passage automatically.

Is my typing history saved?

Results are kept on the Dashboard for the current browser session so you can track progress across attempts. Closing or reloading the page clears the history.

Is this typing test free?

Yes, it is completely free with no signup required.
