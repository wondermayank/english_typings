Frequently Asked Questions

How is WPM calculated?

WPM is calculated as the number of correctly typed characters divided by 5 (the average word length), divided by the time taken in minutes. Typing errors reduce your final WPM.

What happens if I finish before the timer runs out?

If you complete the passage before a countdown timer ends, the result is marked "Finished Early" and shows how many seconds you had to spare.

Can I practice with my own text?

Yes. Choose "Custom Text" to paste your own paragraph, or upload a .txt file and it will be loaded as your passage automatically.

Is my typing history saved?

Yes. Your history, best scores, achievements, XP, and level are saved in your browser using localStorage, so they persist across visits on the same device and browser. Clearing your browser data will reset them.

What is Government Exam Mode?

It mirrors common government typing-test formats — a fixed duration, backspace disabled, and a minimum WPM plus accuracy needed to pass — useful for practicing before recruitment typing tests. Always confirm the exact criteria against your exam's official notification.

What is Ghost Race mode?

Once you have a best score on a passage, you can race a "ghost" of that attempt — a marker that moves through the passage at your previous pace, so you can see in real time whether you're ahead or behind.

Is this typing test free?

Yes, it is completely free with no signup required.
